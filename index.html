What Is OANDA Login and How Can You Use It? A Complete Guide

OANDA login is the authentication process that lets you securely access your registered account with one of the world's most established online forex and CFD brokers. Founded in 1996, OANDA is a regulated broker serving traders in numerous countries. Whether you are a new trader or an experienced one, understanding how OANDA login works across its various platforms is essential before you can manage your funds, open trading positions, or configure your account settings. This guide covers everything you need to know about OANDA login — what it is, how it works, the different login methods, security features, and practical tips for using it effectively. ⁠

What Exactly Is OANDA Login?

OANDA login refers to the process of verifying your identity to gain access to your account. Unlike some brokers that use a single unified login, OANDA operates two distinct credential systems:

HUB / Portal credentials: Your registered email address and password. These are used to log in to the HUB (hub.oanda.com) — the central account management dashboard — or the OANDA Portal (portal.oanda.com) for OANDA Global Markets (BVI) clients. From the HUB you can manage deposits, withdrawals, account details, security settings, and toggle to the trading platforms.
Trading platform credentials: A numeric login ID, a separate trading password, and a server name. These are used to connect to MetaTrader 4 (MT4) or MetaTrader 5 (MT5) on desktop, mobile, or web. ⁠
Confusing these two sets of credentials is the most common cause of login failures. For example, entering your HUB password into MT5 will produce an "Authorization Failed" error because the system expects the trading password instead.

How to Log In to the OANDA HUB
The HUB is the primary login destination for most users. Here is the step-by-step process:

Open your web browser and navigate to hub.oanda.com. Always verify the URL and confirm the padlock icon in the address bar, indicating a secure HTTPS connection. Never enter credentials on a page that does not match this exact domain.
Enter your OANDA email address in the username field. This is the email you used when you first created your account. A trading account number is not a valid HUB username.
Enter your OANDA password. ⁠ The password is case-sensitive and may include numbers and special characters. If your demo and live accounts share the same email, you must use the live account password to sign in. ⁠
Complete two-factor authentication (2FA) if it is enabled on your account. You will be prompted to enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator.
Click "Sign In". You will be directed to the HUB dashboard, where you can manage all aspects of your OANDA account, including funding, account settings, and toggling to the OANDA web trading platform from the top menu.
OANDA Web Platform Login
The OANDA web trading platform is accessed at trade.oanda.com. ⁠ You can log in directly there using the same email and password as the HUB, or you can log in to the HUB first and then toggle to "TRADE" from the top navigation menu.

OANDA Mobile App Login
The OANDA mobile app (available on both Android and iOS) uses the same credentials as the HUB. Open the app, tap "Sign In," and enter your registered email and password. For OANDA Global Markets (BVI) clients, the app may prompt you to use a one-key login option — follow the on-screen instructions to authorize the connection. ⁠

MT4 and MT5 Desktop Login
MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 use entirely different credentials from the HUB. To log in:

Open the MT4 or MT5 application on your computer.
Click File → Login to Trade Account (MT4) or File → Open an Account (MT5).
Enter your numeric login ID (found in the HUB under your account details, not your email).
Enter your trading password (not your HUB password).
Select the correct server (e.g., "OANDA-MT5" or similar, as shown in the HUB).
Click Login.
If you see an "Authorization Failed" message, the most likely cause is using the wrong password type or the wrong server. You can reset your MT4/MT5 password from the HUB. ⁠

Security Features of OANDA Login
OANDA provides several layers of protection to keep your account secure:

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): You can enable 2FA through Profile settings in the HUB. The recommended method uses Google Authenticator, a free app that generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP). ⁠ Once enabled, you will need to enter a verification code every time you log in (unless you select "Remember this device for 30 days"). Notably, for clients in Singapore, 2FA became a mandatory regulatory requirement effective September 12, 2025, under MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore) rules. ⁠ Clients in that region cannot disable 2FA once enabled.
Password Requirements: OANDA passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, at least one digit, and a special character. This reduces the risk of brute-force attacks. ⁠
Passkey Support: OANDA now supports passkey-based login as an alternative to traditional passwords, adding a modern biometric or device-based authentication option.
Phishing Protection: OANDA will never ask for your password, OTP, or financial details through email, Telegram, WhatsApp, or any messaging platform. The only legitimate login pages are hub.oanda.com, trade.oanda.com, and portal.oanda.com. If you receive an email with a login link claiming to be from OANDA, do not click it — go directly to the official URL instead.
Can You Use OANDA Login from India?
This is a critical question for Indian traders, and the answer has been evolving.

If you already have an OANDA account: You can log in and trade normally. Existing accounts remain fully functional, and you can access the HUB, deposit and withdraw funds, and trade on the OANDA web platform or MetaTrader without any restrictions.

If you are in India and want to open a new account: The situation is mixed. Some recent reviews and broker comparison sites (including FXScouts and BrokerRank, both updated in late 2026) indicate that OANDA Global Markets Ltd (registered in the British Virgin Islands) does accept clients from India, with accounts typically ready within one day. ⁠ However, other sources (FXEmpire, FxBrokersListing, and BrokerChooser) list India as a restricted country where OANDA does not accept new registrations. ⁠

This discrepancy likely stems from the fact that OANDA operates through different legal entities in different regions, and availability can change. If you are in India, the safest approach is to check the OANDA registration page directly and see whether your country is listed as eligible. Be aware that even if registration is possible, funding an international trading account from India falls under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), and domestic payment methods like UPI are generally not supported. ⁠ Bank transfers are the typical funding method, and some Indian banks may block such transactions. ⁠

Eligibility Requirements for new registrations in supported countries typically include:

Being at least 18 years old
A valid government-issued identification document (passport, national ID, or driver's license)
Proof of residence (utility bill, bank statement, or similar document)
A valid email address and phone number
Common Login Issues and How to Fix Them
Even experienced traders sometimes run into login problems. Here are the most frequent issues and their solutions:

Forgot your HUB password: Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the sign-in page. OANDA will send a password reset link to your registered email address. Click the link, set a new password, and use it to log in to both the HUB and trading platforms. ⁠
"Authorization Failed" in MT4/MT5: You are likely using your HUB password instead of your trading password, or you selected the wrong server. Check your account details in the HUB for the correct login ID, password, and server name.
2FA code not working: Make sure your device's clock is synchronized, as TOTP codes are time-sensitive. If you have lost access to your authenticator app, contact OANDA support to reset your 2FA method.
Login page looks wrong or different: You may be on a phishing site. Always type hub.oanda.com or trade.oanda.com manually into your browser. Never log in through links in emails, social media messages, or search advertisements.
One-key login error on mobile: If you are using the OANDA app and see an error with the one-key login option, switch to the "Email address and Password" sign-in method instead. ⁠
Tips for a Smooth OANDA Login Experience
Bookmark the official URLs (hub.oanda.com, trade.oanda.com) in your browser for quick and safe access.
Enable 2FA as soon as possible after registration. It is the single most effective step you can take to protect your account.
Keep your Google Authenticator recovery key safe. If you lose your phone or the app, this key is the only way to recover access without contacting support.
Do not reuse your OANDA password on other websites. Use a unique, strong password for your trading account.
Log out of all devices when you are done, especially on shared or public computers.
Check for planned maintenance on OANDA's website before troubleshooting login issues, as scheduled maintenance can temporarily interrupt authentication services. ⁠

Conclusion

OANDA login is a straightforward but important process that serves as the gateway to your entire trading experience with the broker. By understanding the distinction between HUB credentials and trading platform credentials, enabling two-factor authentication, and always verifying that you are on the official login page, you can keep your account secure and your trading experience smooth. For Indian users, the key takeaway is that while availability for new registrations has been inconsistent across sources, existing accounts remain fully operational. Always verify your eligibility directly on the OANDA website before attempting to register, and consult the OANDA Help Centre for region-specific guidance.
